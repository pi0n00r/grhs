# Contributing

GRHS 0.4.0 is open for technical review. Useful contributions identify a reproducible linguistic, encoding, input, search, collation, rendering, accessibility, or editorial issue.

## Before opening an issue

1. Quote the exact section, grapheme ID, test vector, or file involved.
2. State whether the concern is normative, empirical, editorial, or implementation-specific.
3. Provide examples in Unicode text, not screenshots alone.
4. Identify the pronunciation variety or speaker population when making a linguistic claim.
5. Distinguish source-script transliteration from pronunciation-based orthography.

## High-value review

Review is especially requested from:

- Hindi and Urdu phonologists and educators;
- speakers familiar with Delhi/Doabi Hindustani;
- Unicode, ICU, CLDR, keyboard, and search implementers;
- screen-reader and low-vision users or accessibility specialists; and
- readers experienced with Roman Urdu, Hinglish, ALA-LC, ISO 15919, IAST, or Hunterian practice.

## Change control

Changes to canonical spelling require an explicit rationale, test updates, and a major-version decision when incompatible. Mechanical or editorial corrections must not be used to conceal a spelling change.

Contributors certify that they have the right to submit their contribution under `AGPL-3.0-only`.
