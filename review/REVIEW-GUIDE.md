# GRHS 0.4.0 review guide

This review draft needs criticism, not endorsement.

## Linguistic questions

1. Is the declared educated Delhi/Doabi inventory adequate and internally coherent?
2. Is `ñ` for /ŋ/ and `ny` for /ɲ/ usable, or does cross-linguistic expectation create unacceptable confusion?
3. Should `ai` and `au` be analyzed and parsed as single vowel graphemes in this reference variety?
4. Are the reference forms and IPA sample defensible?
5. What lexical or rule-based treatment of schwa deletion can be documented without pretending dialect variation does not exist?
6. How should formal, colloquial, Hindi-leaning, and Urdu-leaning registers interact with stable spelling?

## Reader and education questions

1. Does the acute-accent length convention outperform macrons or familiar informal spellings in learnability?
2. Does U+02B0 `ʰ` avoid ambiguity at an acceptable typing and reading cost?
3. Which distinctions produce the highest error rate for Hindi readers, Urdu readers, bilingual readers, diaspora Roman-script users, and learners?

## Text-technology questions

1. Do NFC normalization and the published code-point sequences round-trip across common platforms?
2. Does longest-match parsing produce unambiguous GRHS graphemes in realistic text?
3. What tailored collation order and search equivalences are justified?
4. Which fonts, keyboards, IMEs, screen readers, and text fields mishandle the repertoire?
5. Which confusable substitutions should be rejected and which should be offered as explicit input conveniences?

## How comments are handled

Every substantive review comment should receive a stable issue ID and a recorded disposition: accepted, accepted with modification, deferred pending evidence, or rejected with rationale. Publication of a comment does not imply that the specification owner accepts it.
